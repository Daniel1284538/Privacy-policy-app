Onboarding walkthrough

Installing the extension & working the PrimeHome Buyers tab

A short, practical guide for backoffice staff: install the PropTech AI Time Management extension, sign in and track your work, then review and send leads to PrimeHome Buyers from the dashboard.

🔗 chromewebstore.google.com/detail/proptech-ai-time-manageme/ejpaamckgoggmegolclnifbmdnbnlmei
01

Install the Chrome extension

The extension is called PropTech AI Time Management — it tracks work time and lets you submit leads straight from the browser toolbar.

1

Open the extension link in Chrome

Click the link above (or paste it into Chrome's address bar). It opens the extension's page on the Chrome Web Store.

2

Click "Add to Chrome"

On the store page you'll see the extension card below. Click the blue button to start installing.

Chrome Web Store

3

Confirm the permissions popup

Chrome will ask you to confirm adding the extension. Click Add extension in that popup — this is normal, Chrome shows it for every extension.

4

Pin it to the toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's toolbar, find PropTech AI Time Management, and click the pin icon so it always stays visible next to your address bar.

02

Sign in and use the extension

The first time you install it, you connect it to your PropTech AI account. After that, you use the small popup every day to track your time and submit leads.

1

Open setup and sign in

Click the extension icon, then Open setup. Your admin gives you the project URL and key once — after that you just sign in with your work email and password every time you switch devices.

2

Pick a task and start tracking

Open the popup, choose your Task from the dropdown, then click Start tracking. The timer runs while you work; a badge always shows whether you're active, idle, or off.

3

Breaks and idle time

Use Take a break when you step away on purpose. If you go idle without clicking it, the extension detects it automatically and asks you to confirm you're back before it resumes tracking.

4

Submit leads from the popup

If your task allows it, a Leads section appears below the timer with a small form and a Submit button — use it to log a lead the moment you get it, without leaving your call.

4

Fill in what PrimeHome Buyers requires

The "Send to PrimeHome Buyers" panel opens with the agent's own submission already filled in. Complete the property and seller-situation fields marked with a red asterisk, then the two fields agents never fill in themselves:

  • Call recording link — paste a URL, or attach the recording file for this lead.
  • Ping ID — paste the ID, or attach the proof file for this lead.

5

Save, then send

Save details stores your progress without sending anything — use it if you're missing something like a recording. Send to PrimeHome Buyers submits the completed lead; every past attempt stays visible underneath in Send History, so you can see exactly what happened if a send ever fails.

6

Bulk import with CSV / Excel

Got a batch of leads instead of one? Switch to the Upload CSV / Excel subtab. Download the template, fill it in (or upload a raw call-intake export — it's auto-detected and converted), check the preview, and import.

  • A row whose phone matches an existing lead fills in that lead's details.
  • A row with no match creates a brand-new lead, credited to you, ready for review in Lead Reviewing.
  • Rows tagged Do-Not-Call are skipped automatically during conversion.

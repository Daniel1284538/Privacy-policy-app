1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When your account is created (by our staff or by you), we collect and store:

  • Full name, email address, company name, and phone number
  • Account type/role and account status
  • Login timestamps and password-change timestamps

1.2 Business and Service Data

As part of using the Service, we collect and store the data you and our team generate through it, including:

  • Real estate leads assigned or delivered to your account, and any comments, disputes, or feedback you submit about them
  • Marketing campaign access, campaign guides, and any edits or requests you submit related to them
  • Skip-trace orders, including files you upload for processing and the results returned
  • "Buy a List" record orders and order history
  • Support tickets and their status/history

1.3 Billing and Payment Information

We track subscription status, pending payments, one-off charges, and skip-trace credit balances associated with your account. We do not collect or store your full payment card details. Card payments are processed by Stripe through Stripe's own secure checkout page, opened in your device's browser — Stripe's handling of your payment data is governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy. We only receive confirmation of payment status from Stripe, not your card number.

1.4 Remi AI Chat and Support Communications

When you use the in-app "Remi AI" assistant or request to speak with a team member, we collect:

  • The text of messages you send and receive
  • Any images or files you choose to attach and send
  • Voice recordings you choose to record and send as voice notes (microphone access is only used when you actively record a voice note; we do not record audio in the background)
  • Message delivery/read status and timestamps

1.5 Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, we use Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service) to deliver push notifications about activity on your account (e.g., new leads, ticket updates, chat replies). This requires storing a device push token associated with your account. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings or in-app notification preferences.

1.6 Biometric Login

If you enable biometric login (fingerprint/face unlock), your biometric data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. It is processed entirely by your device's operating system, which only tells the App "unlock succeeded" or "unlock failed." We never receive or store the biometric data itself.

1.7 Automatically Collected / Diagnostic Information

We use Firebase App Check and reCAPTCHA to help verify that requests to our backend come from a genuine, unmodified copy of the App, in order to prevent abuse and automated attacks. This may involve collecting device-level integrity signals. We do not use this information for advertising or profiling.

1.8 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your device's precise or approximate location, and we do not use advertising SDKs or sell data to data brokers or advertisers.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service (deliver leads, process orders, manage billing, provide support)
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Send you service-related notifications and respond to your support requests
  • Power the Remi AI assistant's responses to your questions
  • Monitor, troubleshoot, and improve the Service
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. How We Share Information

We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, under contractual confidentiality and security obligations:
    • Supabase — our database, authentication, and backend infrastructure provider, which hosts substantially all of the data described in Section 1.
    • Stripe — for payment processing (see Section 1.3).
    • Google Firebase — for push notification delivery and app integrity checks (Sections 1.5 and 1.7).
  • Our staff, who can access client data through the corresponding staff portal in order to deliver leads, respond to support requests, and manage your account.
  • Legal requirements — if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of PropTech AI, our users, or others.
  • Business transfers — if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction; we will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

We do not otherwise sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain account and business data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Chat conversations are retained per Section 1.4 for support and audit purposes even if a conversation is marked as "cleared" from your active view. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data as described in Section 5.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information (you can also update most profile fields directly in the App)
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal information
  • Opt out of push notifications (device settings or in-app notification preferences)
  • Disable biometric login at any time (App settings)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the App and our backend, row-level access controls restricting each client to their own data, and access controls for our staff. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use by real estate professionals and is not directed to, and should not be used by, anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by other reasonable means, and update the "Effective date" above.
